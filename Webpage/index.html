The name "Hida" could be a play on "hide" or "hidden," suggesting that this creature might have stealthy or elusive qualities. The name "Luxin" is derived from the Latin word "lux," meaning "light." This name emphasizes the creature's glowing and ethereal nature. It suggests that Luxin embodies the essence of light, perhaps acting as a beacon or guide within the darkness. The name implies purity, clarity, and an illuminating presence that stands out in the dark.

The glowing leaves hint at a creature that may possess healing or nurturing qualities, illuminating and bringing life to its surroundings.
